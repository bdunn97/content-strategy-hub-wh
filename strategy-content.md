# Wilder Home: Creator Content Strategy

*Brand launch: after the new year. Owner: Marketing. Status: draft for review with Bren.*

## Strategy at a glance

Wilder Home is launching without an existing audience, so creators are our audience, our content engine and our proof. The approach runs in three tiers, each with a different job:

| | Tier 1: Mid Creators | Tier 2: Micro Creators | Tier 3: Macro Creators (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Job** | Content pipeline | Conversion + volume | Third-party validation |
| **What we buy** | Content + usage rights | Posts + sales | Awareness |
| **Structure** | Paid contract | Trade + commission (affiliate) | Paid awareness contract |
| **Rights** | Organic in perpetuity; 1 yr paid, renewable at same rate | Defined in affiliate terms (see open items) | Minimal; awareness-first |
| **Volume** | Core roster (size TBD with budget) | 3-5 creators | 1-2, only if budget allows |
| **Priority** | Must-have | Must-have | Nice-to-have |

**Guiding principle:** Tier 1 creates the content we scale through paid social, Tier 2 proves what converts, and Tier 3 (if funded) lends credibility once there is something worth validating.

---

## Tier 1: Mid Creators (content pipeline)

**Goal:** Build a repeatable pipeline of high-quality, on-brand creator content that powers both organic channels and creator-led paid social ads.

**Why mid-sized creators:** They typically deliver strong production quality and engaged audiences at a cost far below macro creators, and they are professional enough to work to a brief, hit deadlines and sign usage terms.

### Vetting criteria
- **Brand and direction fit:** Their aesthetic and point of view match where Wilder Home is going, not just where home content is today. Review the last 30-60 days of posts, not only their top hits.
- **Content quality:** Strong lighting, clear product-in-context shots, a recognizable editing style. Their content should be usable as an ad with minimal reworking.
- **Engagement quality:** Real comments and saves over raw follower count. Look for audience conversation about products, not generic replies.
- **Audience match:** Home-focused followers, US-based where possible.
- **Professionalism:** Responsive, has worked with brands, comfortable with briefs and revision rounds.
- **Brand safety:** No conflicting recent partnerships in the category, no content that clashes with the brand.
- **Ad-readiness:** Willing to grant paid usage and, if needed, whitelisting/partnership ad access.

### Sourcing
- Build the master list in the Creators tab, tagged by tier, niche, aesthetic and status.
- Source through Instagram/TikTok search and hashtags, Pinterest, saved-by-audience lookalikes, competitor creator partnerships (from the ad library work), and creator marketplaces.
- Score each creator against the vetting criteria and keep a short list with a clear yes / maybe / no.

### Contract terms (standard)
- **Organic usage rights:** in perpetuity.
- **Paid usage rights:** 1 year from delivery date.
- **Renewal:** option to renew paid usage at the same rate.
- Defined deliverables per creator (e.g. number of videos/photos), revision rounds, due dates, and approval process.
- FTC-compliant disclosure on all posts.
- Exclusivity window in the home category to be decided.

### Content pipeline
1. **Brief:** shared creative brief with brand direction, key messages, do's/don'ts and shot ideas, with room for the creator's own voice.
2. **Seed:** product shipped ahead of the content window.
3. **Draft review:** 1-2 consolidated feedback rounds.
4. **Deliver + tag:** files organized by creator, format, product and rights expiry date.
5. **Deploy:** organic posts, brand channels, and top performers go into paid social.
6. **Learn + renew:** use performance data to decide which creators get more work and which usage rights to renew.

### Success measures
- Number of usable assets delivered per creator
- Share of assets that pass into paid social
- Paid performance of creator content versus any non-creator content
- Cost per usable asset
- Renewal rate of top performers

## Tier 2: Micro Creators (trade + commission)

**Goal:** Drive sales and a steady stream of authentic posts through 3-5 micro creators on a trade + commission (affiliate) basis.

**Why micro creators:** Strong micro audiences tend to convert better than macro audiences because the trust is higher and the relationship feels personal. Their smaller following also gives them more reason to post often, so we get volume and variety without day-rate costs.

### Structure
- **Trade:** product in exchange for content.
- **Commission:** affiliate link or code, with commission on sales they drive.
- Start with **3-5 creators**, which keeps the approach manageable and gives us clean learnings.

### Selection criteria
- Proven buying audience: comments asking where to buy, past affiliate success, product-focused content.
- Posts frequently and consistently.
- Genuine fit with Wilder Home products and direction.
- Comfortable working on affiliate terms.

### How we make it work
- Give each creator a unique code and link so sales are attributed cleanly.
- Provide a simple kit: brand story, key product points, talking points, and imagery guidelines.
- Offer a clear path to grow: top performers can be moved into Tier 1 paid work.
- Keep communication light and regular rather than heavily directive.

### Success measures
- Attributed sales and revenue per creator
- Conversion rate on code/link traffic
- Posts per creator per month
- Content that is strong enough to reuse (with rights)

---

## Tier 3 (optional): Macro Creators (third-party validation)

**Goal:** Use a small number of larger creators to give the brand third-party credibility and awareness at launch or just after.

### Approach
- Contracts are focused on **awareness and reach**, not on obtaining content rights.
- Pursue only if budget allows after Tier 1 and 2 are secured.
- Prioritize creators whose audience trust is real and whose taste signals quality in the home space.
- Time activations to build on proof we already have from Tiers 1 and 2 (reviews, content, early sales), so validation lands on a brand that looks established.

### Success measures
- Reach and engagement on the placement
- Lift in branded search, profile visits and follower growth
- Traffic and sales in the days after posting
- Use of the placement as social proof in our own channels (where permitted)

## Suggested phasing

| Phase | Timing | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Build** | Now through December | Finalize creator list, vetting scores, contract template, brief and product seeding plan |
| **2. Outreach + contracting** | December | Reach out to Tier 1 and Tier 2 creators, negotiate and sign, ship product |
| **3. Content production** | Late December through launch | Creators produce, review rounds, asset library organized |
| **4. Launch** | After the new year | Organic posts go live, Tier 2 codes activate, first paid creative goes into paid social |
| **5. Optimize + expand** | First 60-90 days | Double down on top performers, renew rights, graduate micros, decide on Tier 3 |

*Dates are directional and should be adjusted to the confirmed launch date.*

---

## How the tiers work together

- **Tier 1 feeds paid social:** the rights we negotiate are what let us run creator content as ads.
- **Tier 2 feeds learning:** affiliate data shows which products, hooks and creator types actually convert.
- **Tier 3 feeds credibility:** validation is most valuable once there is content and proof to point to.
- Insights from every tier flow back into the briefs for the next round.

---

## Open items to decide

1. **Budget:** total creator budget and split across tiers.
2. **Tier 2 usage rights:** since we plan creator-led paid ads, affiliate terms should state whether we can reuse their content organically and/or in paid, and for how long. Trade-only agreements often do not include this by default.
3. **Commission rate** for the affiliate program.
4. **Tier 1 rate ranges** and number of deliverables per creator.
5. **Exclusivity:** how long, and in which categories.
6. **Whitelisting/partnership ad access:** include in contracts if ads will run from creator handles.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Brand has no audience at launch | Tier 1 content is built for the brand's own channels and ads, not dependent on creator reach |
| Creator content does not match brand direction | Strong brief plus draft review round; vetting on direction, not just popularity |
| Rights expire unnoticed | Track rights expiry dates in the asset library and set renewal reminders |
| Micro creators underdeliver | Start with 3-5, clear posting expectations, option to replace quickly |
| Disclosure or compliance gaps | Require FTC-compliant disclosure in every contract and brief |
| Macro spend does not pay back | Keep Tier 3 optional and gated behind early proof points |
